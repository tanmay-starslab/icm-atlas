How to read

  • Rows are lines, cool to hot. Columns are instrument cases (Layout).
  • Flux is normalised to the continuum; v is the velocity relative to the cluster's systemic velocity.
  • Stepped line: the degraded spectrum, a Gaussian line-spread function of FWHM c/R with 5 pixels and the given S/N per resolution element. Shading: the noiseless model. Intrinsic (dashed): the noiseless spectrum before the line-spread function.
  • Fitted components: the fitted model in ink, each component in the line colour and a tick at its centre. A hollow tick marks a component on a bound, cut by the time cap, or unconstrained (log N error above 1 dex). An arrow marks a saturated component: its log N is only a lower limit, read as “log N > 14.5 sat”. The readout gives the fitted values, their errors and flags.
  • Sightline orientations are defined relative to the cluster's principal inertia / angular-momentum axis (face-on along the axis, edge-on in the cluster midplane, or random 3D direction) across impact parameters b = 0 to 4000 kpc (±4 Mpc).
  • Drag or use the arrow keys to pan; ctrl or cmd with the wheel, or + and −, zoom; Home resets.
  • 3D: drag to orbit across 5 nested octree levels (±4 Mpc L0 down to ±15 kpc L4 BCG core), scroll to zoom, click a sightline to open its spectra.

Data and methods

Sightlines through the intracluster medium (ICM) of 382 massive galaxy clusters at z = 0: 352 TNG-Cluster zoom-in halos (M200c ∼ 1014.3–1015.4 M⊙, Nelson et al. 2024), 10 SIMBA-100 massive clusters (Davé et al. 2019), and 20 TNG300-1 clusters (Pillepich et al. 2018; Nelson et al. 2019), spanning ±4 Mpc (L0) down to ±15 kpc BCG cores (L4).

Optical depths from 120-ray THOR Voronoi mesh ray-tracing + FP64 Voigt synthesis (or Trident on a 0.25 km/s grid), stored at 1 km/s over ±1500 km/s across impact parameters up to 4 Mpc. Velocities are relative to the cluster's systemic velocity.

120-ray sightline libraries traced through the native Voronoi/SPH mesh with THOR (Byrohl & Nelson 2025) and CLOUDY (Ferland et al. 2017) FG11 UV/X-ray background ionisation tables (Faucher-Giguère et al. 2009) with Rahmati et al. (2013) H I self-shielding, plus Trident HM2012 (Haardt & Madau 2012) across all 12 transitions (H I, Mg II, Si II, C II, Si III, C III, Si IV, C IV, N V, O VI, Ne VIII, O VII).

Atomic data follow Trident 1.4.2 and Morton (2003) (H I Lyα Γ = 6.265 × 108 s−1, Wiese & Fuhr 2009; O VII 21.602 Å from Verner et al. 1996). Saturated components are lower limits.

The degradation and the Voigt fits run in your browser: Levenberg–Marquardt on (log N, b, v) through the line-spread function, seeded from the intrinsic component catalogue and pruned by ΔBIC.

5-Level 3D ICM Octree: every gas cell and star particle is deposited into five nested 256³ cubes covering ±4 Mpc (L0, 31.25 kpc voxels) → ±1 Mpc (L1, 7.8 kpc voxels) → ±250 kpc (L2, 1.95 kpc voxels) → ±62.5 kpc (L3, 488 pc voxels) → ±15 kpc (L4, 117 pc BCG core voxels), plus BCG and satellite starlight cubes S0..S4 (SDSS g, r, i Lupton asinh composite with 3D dust radiative transfer), streamed live from public Hugging Face datasets (tanmay-starslab/icm-atlas-3d-0, icm-atlas-3d-1, icm-atlas-web-0, and icm-atlas-web-1). 3D velocities are mass-weighted means per voxel in the cluster rest frame (vx, vy and vz each clipped at the top of the 3D velocity window).

Data and credits

Simulations (382 Galaxy Clusters at z = 0)

TNG-Cluster (352 zoom-in clusters): IllustrisTNG TNG-Cluster at z = 0 (snapshot 99, all 352 massive galaxy cluster zoom-in halos with M200c ∼ 1014.3–1015.4 M⊙ drawn from a 1 Gpc³ parent volume; Nelson et al. 2024, A&A 686, A157), from the TNG public data release.

SIMBA-100 (10 massive clusters): SIMBA m100n1024 cosmological hydrodynamic simulation at z = 0 (snapshot 151, top 10 most massive galaxy clusters, ±4 Mpc cutouts; Davé et al. 2019, MNRAS 486, 2827), from the SIMBA public data release, run with GIZMO meshless finite-mass hydrodynamics and two-mode AGN jet/radiative feedback.

TNG300-1 (20 clusters & rich groups): IllustrisTNG TNG300-1 302.6 Mpc cosmological volume at z = 0 (snapshot 99, 20 massive clusters and rich groups; Pillepich et al. 2018, MNRAS 475, 648; Nelson et al. 2019, ComAC 6, 2; Springel et al. 2018).

We thank the IllustrisTNG and SIMBA collaborations for making these simulations public.

Spectra & 5-Level 3D ICM Cubes

120-ray cluster sightline libraries traced with THOR (Byrohl & Nelson 2025, arXiv:2507.11603) and CLOUDY FG11 (Ferland et al. 2017; Faucher-Giguère et al. 2009; Rahmati et al. 2013) plus Trident (Hummels, Smith and Silvia 2017, ApJ 847, 59) on yt (Turk et al. 2011, ApJS 192, 9) with the HM2012 UV/X-ray background (Haardt and Madau 2012, ApJ 746, 125). 5-level 256³ 3D ICM cubes cover ±4 Mpc (L0, 31.25 kpc voxels) → ±1 Mpc (L1, 7.8 kpc) → ±250 kpc (L2, 1.95 kpc) → ±62.5 kpc (L3, 488 pc) → ±15 kpc (L4, 117 pc BCG core), plus BCG and satellite starlight cubes S0..S4.
